Questions, answered.

How does the waitlist work?

Add your email and we'll let you in as spots open. When you're approved we email you an invitation to set a password and take the questionnaire. Early access is free.

Will it cost anything?

Not during early access, and you never need a card to join. There are never any application fees.

What do you do with my answers?

They build your matching profile. We don't sell them or use them for ads. If you remove a match and tell us why, we use that, with the parts of your profile that matter for that award, to improve matching. You can ask us to delete your account at any time. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

How accurate are the matches?

Every award links to the provider's own page, where we read the details from. For now those details are read automatically and not yet checked by a person, so awards are labeled "Unverified details" and some requirements can be missed. Always confirm the deadline and the rules on the provider's page before you apply. If something looks wrong, tell us from your dashboard. We are adding human checks over time.

Do I have to answer everything?

No. Only a short core block is required. Everything personal is optional, and skipping is never treated as a "no".

Does it apply for me?

No. You keep control of your applications. We help you find awards; you decide what's worth your time and apply with the provider.
