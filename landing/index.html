Questions, answered.

How much does it cost?

Your first 7 days are free. After that it is $9 a month or $60 for the year. We email you before the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime. There are never any application fees.

What do you do with my answers?

They build your matching profile and nothing else. We don't sell them, and you can delete your profile at any time.

How do you know a scholarship is real?

Each award is traced back to the official source page, then checked for a live deadline, clear eligibility rules and no fees before it reaches you.

Do I have to answer everything?

No. Only a short core block is required. Everything personal is optional, and skipping is never treated as a "no".

Does it apply for me?

No. You keep control of your applications. We find and verify; you decide what's worth your time.
